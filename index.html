01 — SURVEY BRIEF

A world that is
almost all water.

Ninety-four per cent of Meridian's surface is liquid under an unbroken cloud shell eleven kilometres deep. The remaining six carries every light we have counted — 2,180 discrete emission clusters, all of them on the night side, all of them inside the same forty-degree band.

01Terminator crossing logged at 03:11:44 UTC. Cloud deck holds at eleven kilometres. No convective towers on this hemisphere, in this season, at this latitude.

02The emission clusters resolve into filaments. They follow rivers, not coastlines. Whatever is down there builds along water and then stops.

03Thermal delta between the lit and unlit hemispheres: 61 K. Modest, for a world with an axial tilt we cannot measure.

04Nothing answers on any hailing band across four passes. The survey continues. It is not our function to conclude.

The Long Cut

A single canyon system runs 4,180 km along the equator and never exceeds 9 km in width. Erosion cannot do this. Nothing else we have imaged does it either.

Polar Cap

Remanent Field

No active dynamo. The crust remembers one anyway — banded magnetisation in the southern highlands, 800 nT, frozen in place some three billion years before we arrived to read it.
